# Wireless Metropolitan Area Network (WMAN)
# 📖 Overview

A **Wireless Metropolitan Area Network (WMAN)** is a wireless network that provides connectivity across a **metropolitan area**, such as a city or large town.

A WMAN is larger than a **Wireless Local Area Network (WLAN)** but smaller than a **Wireless Wide Area Network (WWAN)**. It is commonly used to connect multiple buildings, campuses, offices, or neighborhoods without relying entirely on wired infrastructure.

One of the most well-known technologies used in WMANs is **WiMAX (Worldwide Interoperability for Microwave Access)**.

# 🌐 What is WMAN?

A **Wireless Metropolitan Area Network (WMAN)** is a wireless network that connects users and organizations across a city or metropolitan region.

Instead of connecting only devices within a single building, a WMAN can interconnect multiple LANs or WLANs over a much larger geographic area.

---

# ⚙️ How WMAN Works

A WMAN typically operates using high-powered wireless base stations.

The communication process is generally as follows:

1. A central base station transmits wireless signals.
2. Nearby buildings or customer equipment receive these signals.
3. The connected networks exchange data through the WMAN infrastructure.
4. Users gain access to shared resources or the Internet.

---

# 📏 Coverage Area

Typical WMAN coverage:

```text
Approximately 5 to 50 kilometers
```

Coverage depends on:

- Terrain
- Antenna height
- Weather conditions
- Wireless technology used

---

# 📡 Common WMAN Technologies

Common technologies include:

- WiMAX (IEEE 802.16)
- Metropolitan wireless broadband systems

> WiMAX was designed to provide broadband wireless connectivity over metropolitan areas.

---

# ✅ Advantages

- Covers a much larger area than WLAN.
- Supports multiple buildings and campuses.
- Reduces the need for extensive wired infrastructure.
- Can provide broadband Internet access.
- Suitable for urban deployments.

---

# ❌ Limitations

- Higher deployment cost than WLAN.
- Requires specialized infrastructure.
- Performance may be affected by obstacles and weather.
- Less common today than cellular broadband in many regions.

---

# 🌍 Real-World Applications

WMAN is commonly used for:

- Connecting multiple office buildings
- University campuses
- Government networks
- City-wide Internet services
- Metropolitan broadband connectivity
