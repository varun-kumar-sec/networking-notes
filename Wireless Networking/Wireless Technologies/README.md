# Wireless Technologies

> Learn about the most common wireless communication technologies used in modern computer networks and understand their characteristics, applications, and differences.

---

# 📖 Overview

Wireless technologies enable devices to communicate **without physical cables** by transmitting data through radio waves.

Different wireless technologies are designed for different purposes. Some provide high-speed Internet access, while others focus on low-power communication between nearby devices or were developed for specialized home networking.

This section introduces three important wireless technologies:

- **Wi-Fi**
- **Bluetooth**
- **HomeRF**

Understanding these technologies helps you choose the appropriate wireless solution for different networking environments.

---

# 🎯 Learning Objectives

After completing this section, you will be able to:

- Understand the purpose of common wireless technologies.
- Differentiate between Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, and HomeRF.
- Identify the advantages and limitations of each technology.
- Recognize where each technology is commonly used.

# 📊 Comparison of Wireless Technologies

| Technology | Primary Purpose | Typical Range | Speed | Common Applications |
|------------|-----------------|---------------|-------|----------------------|
| Wi-Fi | Wireless networking | Medium | High | Internet access, home and office networks |
| Bluetooth | Personal device communication | Short | Moderate | Headphones, keyboards, smartwatches |
| HomeRF | Home wireless networking (Legacy) | Short | Low | Early home networking (obsolete) |

# 💡 Key Differences

- **Wi-Fi** is designed for high-speed wireless networking and Internet access.
- **Bluetooth** is optimized for short-range, low-power communication between personal devices.
- **HomeRF** was an early home wireless networking technology that has since become obsolete.

---

# 📌 Key Takeaways

- Wireless technologies enable communication without physical cables.
- Different technologies are designed for different networking requirements.
- **Wi-Fi** is the standard for wireless local area networking.
- **Bluetooth** is commonly used for connecting nearby personal devices.
- **HomeRF** is a legacy technology that has been replaced by Wi-Fi.
